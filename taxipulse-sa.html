Privacy Policy — TaxiPulse SA

Effective Date: October 25, 2026
Developer & Publisher: Barlow on Trust
Android Package Name: com.barlowontrust.taxipulsesa / com.barlowontrust.taxipulse

1. Overview & Core Privacy Commitment

TaxiPulse SA ("TaxiPulse SA: Ranks & ZAR Fare") is built by Barlow on Trust as a 100% offline-first commuter utility for South African minibus taxi passengers across Johannesburg, Cape Town, Durban, and Hazyview (Mpumalanga). All rank queue lookups, ZAR route fare calculations, front-seat row change math, and commuter safety registration plate logs run strictly locally on your Android device.

2. Information We Do NOT Collect

  • No GPS or Background Location Tracking: TaxiPulse SA does not request, access, track, or transmit your device's GPS coordinates or physical location. You manually select your metropolitan hub inside the app.
  • No Personal Data or Account Registration: You never need to create an account, sign in, or provide your name, phone number, or email address to use TaxiPulse SA.
  • No Cloud Uploads of Registration Plate Logs: Any minibus taxi registration numbers or trip notes you enter into the Commuter Safety Check-In tool are stored strictly on your device and are never uploaded to external servers.
  • No Third-Party Ad Trackers or Analytics SDKs: TaxiPulse SA contains zero advertising SDKs, analytics trackers, or data brokers.

3. Local Device Storage & User Control

TaxiPulse SA uses standard on-device storage (localStorage) solely to remember your selected metro hub, local queue status logs, and Ubuntu Karma points between sessions. You can reset or clear this data at any time using the "Reset Demo Data" button inside the app or by clearing storage in Android Settings.

4. Commute Safety Sharing (WhatsApp / SMS)

When you tap "Share via WhatsApp / SMS" in the Safety Check-In section, TaxiPulse SA hands a pre-formatted plain-text message to your phone's native Android share sheet or messaging app only at your explicit request. TaxiPulse SA never sends messages automatically or accesses your contacts list.

5. Children's Privacy

TaxiPulse SA does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children under the age of 13.
