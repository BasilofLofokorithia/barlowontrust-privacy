Privacy Policy — TaxiPulse SA

1. Overview & Core Privacy Commitment

TaxiPulse SA ("TaxiPulse SA: Ranks & ZAR Fare") is built by Barlow on Trust as a 100% offline-first commuter utility for South African minibus taxi passengers across Johannesburg, Cape Town, Durban, and Hazyview (Mpumalanga). All rank queue lookups, ZAR route fare calculations, front-seat row change math, and commuter safety registration plate logs run strictly locally on your Android device.

2. Information We Do NOT Collect

  • No GPS or Background Location Tracking: TaxiPulse SA does not request, access, track, or transmit your device's GPS coordinates or physical location. You manually select your metropolitan hub inside the app.
  • No Personal Data or Account Registration: You never need to create an account, sign in, or provide your name, South African ID number, phone number, or email address to use TaxiPulse SA.
  • No Cloud Uploads of Registration Plate Logs: Any minibus taxi registration numbers or trip notes you enter into the Commuter Safety Check-In tool are stored strictly on your device and are never uploaded to external servers.
  • No Third-Party Ad Trackers or Analytics SDKs: TaxiPulse SA contains zero advertising SDKs, analytics trackers, or data brokers.

3. South African POPIA Compliance (Protection of Personal Information Act, Act 4 of 2013)

TaxiPulse SA is specifically engineered to uphold the privacy rights of South African commuters under the Protection of Personal Information Act, 2013 (Act No. 4 of 2013) ("POPIA"):

  • Zero Personal Information Processing by the Responsible Party (Conditions 1–7, Sections 8–22): Because TaxiPulse SA operates 100% offline on the user's Android device with zero accounts, zero cloud servers, zero GPS tracking, and zero analytics SDKs, Barlow on Trust does not collect, receive, record, store, or process any "personal information" (as defined in Section 1 of POPIA) on external servers.
  • No Cross-Border Transfer of Personal Information (Section 72): Because all rank selections, ZAR fare calculations, front-seat change math, and commuter safety registration plate logs remain strictly inside local device storage (localStorage) on the commuter's phone in South Africa, no personal information is ever transmitted or transferred outside the Republic of South Africa.
  • Data Subject Rights & On-Device Erasure (Condition 8, Sections 23–25): Users ("data subjects") retain 100% direct physical and technical control over their on-device logs and can inspect, modify, or permanently erase all local app data at any time in one tap via the in-app "Reset Demo Data" control or Android system settings.
  • Information Officer & South African Information Regulator Contact: For any POPIA-related inquiries, you may contact the Information Officer at Barlow on Trust (e.v.barlow.it@gmail.com). South African users also hold the statutory right to contact the Information Regulator (South Africa) at inforegulator.org.za or enquiries@inforegulator.org.za.

4. Local Device Storage & User Control

TaxiPulse SA uses standard on-device storage (localStorage) solely to remember your selected metro hub, local queue status logs, and Ubuntu Karma points between sessions. You can reset or clear this data at any time using the "Reset Demo Data" button inside the app or by clearing storage in Android Settings.

5. Commute Safety Sharing (WhatsApp / SMS)

When you tap "Share via WhatsApp / SMS" in the Safety Check-In section, TaxiPulse SA hands a pre-formatted plain-text message to your phone's native Android share sheet or messaging app only at your explicit request. TaxiPulse SA never sends messages automatically or accesses your contacts list.

6. Children's Privacy

TaxiPulse SA does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children under the age of 18 (as defined under POPIA) or under the age of 13.
