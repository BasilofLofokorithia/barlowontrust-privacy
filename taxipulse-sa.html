Privacy Policy — TaxiPulse ZA

Effective Date: November 25, 2026 (replaces the October 27, 2026 version)
Developer & Publisher (Responsible Party): Erich Barlow, LLC
Android Package Name: com.erichbarlowllc.taxipulseza
Earlier closed-test builds, published as TaxiPulse SA, used com.barlowontrust.taxipulsesa; this policy covers them too.

1. Overview

TaxiPulse ZA is an offline-first commuter utility for South African minibus taxi passengers in Johannesburg, Cape Town, Durban, and Hazyview (Mpumalanga). Rank queue status, ZAR route fares, front-seat change math, and the commute safety check-in run on your Android device.

2. Information the app does not collect

  • No location tracking: TaxiPulse ZA does not request or use your device's GPS or location permission. You choose your metro in the app.
  • No accounts: You do not create an account or give us your name, South African ID number, phone number, or email address.
  • No uploads of your entries: Taxi registration numbers, trip notes, and queue reports you enter stay on your device. They are not sent to us or to any server.
  • No ads, analytics, or data brokers: TaxiPulse ZA contains no advertising SDKs, shows no ads, and contains no analytics trackers or data brokers.

3. Fare updates (network use)

When your phone is online, the app downloads a public fare and rank list (a JSON file) from GitHub Pages (basiloflofokorithia.github.io, with a fallback on raw.githubusercontent.com). The request contains no information you entered in the app. As with any web request, GitHub, which may process requests outside South Africa, receives your device's IP address and basic request details to deliver the file. We do not receive, log, or combine that information. If you are offline, the app uses the fares saved on your device.

4. South African POPIA (Protection of Personal Information Act 4 of 2013)

  • Processing by the responsible party: Erich Barlow, LLC does not collect, receive, or store personal information from TaxiPulse ZA on any server. Your entries stay in local storage on your phone.
  • Cross-border processing (section 72): We transfer no personal information. The only network request is the public fare-list download described in section 3, which GitHub handles to deliver the file.
  • Your control (sections 23–25): You can view, change, or erase the app's data at any time with the in-app reset control, by clearing the app's storage in Android Settings, or by uninstalling the app.
  • Information Officer: Erich Barlow, Erich Barlow, LLC, erich@erichbarlowllc.com. You may also contact the Information Regulator (South Africa) at inforegulator.org.za or enquiries@inforegulator.org.za.

5. Local storage

TaxiPulse ZA uses on-device storage to remember your selected metro, saved fares, queue reports, and app settings between sessions.

6. Commute safety sharing (WhatsApp / SMS)

When you tap Share in the app, TaxiPulse ZA passes a plain-text message to your phone's Android share sheet or messaging app, and only when you ask it to. The app never sends messages by itself and does not access your contacts.

7. Children's privacy

TaxiPulse ZA is not directed at children and does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children under 18 (as defined in POPIA) or under 13.

8. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, for example if advertising is added in a future version, we will update this page and the effective date before that version is released.
