1. Overview

TrustCheck ("TrustCheck", "the app") checks messages, photos, and phone-call situations for common scam and manipulation signs. Message, photo, and call checks run on your Android device. The app does not upload the text you paste, the photos you choose, or your family code word. Results are rule-based readings, not proof that an item is safe or authentic.

2. Information the app does not collect

  • Messages: Text you paste or share into TrustCheck is checked on your device and is not sent to us or to any server.
  • Photos: Photos you pick with the Android photo picker are inspected on your device and are not uploaded.
  • Family code word: Entries in the Safe-Word Vault stay in the app's local storage on your device.
  • Accounts: There is no TrustCheck account or sign-in. We do not ask for your name, email address, or phone number.
  • Ads and trackers: TrustCheck contains no advertising SDKs, analytics trackers, or data brokers.

3. Local storage and your control

TrustCheck stores your saved scan history, theme preference, weekly free photo-check count, the Safe-Word Vault, and a cached copy of your subscription status on your device. You can clear the history with the Clear button in the app, or remove all app data by clearing storage or uninstalling the app in Android Settings.

4. Subscriptions and payments (Google Play)

TrustCheck is free to download. Optional subscriptions, TrustCheck Pro (monthly) and Family Shield (yearly, with a 7-day free trial for eligible new subscribers), unlock unlimited photo checks. Prices are shown in the app and on Google Play in your local currency.

  • Purchases are processed by Google Play. Erich Barlow, LLC never receives or stores your card number or billing details.
  • To show which plan you have, the app asks Google Play which TrustCheck subscription the Google account on the device owns. A subscription works on Android devices signed in to that same Google account.
  • Google Play's billing library may send purchase details and diagnostic information to Google so purchases work. Google handles that information under the Google Privacy Policy. As the developer, we see standard Google Play order records (such as order ID, product, date, and country) in Google Play Console. We use them only to run and support subscriptions, issue refunds, and meet tax and accounting duties.
  • Subscriptions renew automatically until cancelled. You can cancel or manage them at any time in Google Play (Menu → Payments & subscriptions) or with Manage subscription in the app.

5. Children's privacy

TrustCheck is not directed at children and does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children under 13.
