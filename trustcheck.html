Privacy Policy — TrustCheck: AI & Deepfake Detector

Effective Date: October 24, 2026
Developer & Publisher: Barlow on Trust
Android Package Name: com.barlowontrust.trustcheck

1. Overview & Core Privacy Commitment

TrustCheck: AI & Deepfake Detector ("TrustCheck") is engineered from the ground up as a privacy-first, 100% on-device mobile utility. Unlike cloud-based scanners that upload your private text messages, screenshots, or family photos to external servers, TrustCheck performs all linguistic, metadata, C2PA, and pixel Error Level Analysis (ELA) computations locally in the memory of your Android device.

2. Information We Do NOT Collect

  • No Text or Message Uploads: SMS messages, emails, WhatsApp messages, and voicemail transcripts pasted or shared into TrustCheck are analyzed strictly on your device and are never transmitted over the internet.
  • No Photo or Media Uploads: Photos and screenshots selected via the Android system photo picker are inspected locally via HTML5 Canvas and binary header parsing. Your images never leave your phone.
  • No Safe-Word Transmission: Entries saved inside your Family Safe-Word Vault are stored solely in your device's local application storage (localStorage).
  • No Third-Party Ad Trackers: TrustCheck contains zero third-party advertising SDKs, behavioral trackers, or data brokers.

3. Local Device Storage & User Control

To provide convenience, TrustCheck stores your recent scan log (up to 15 summary entries), theme preference, and Family Safe-Word Vault locally on your device. You can permanently clear this local history at any time using the "Clear Log" button inside the app or by clearing app storage in Android Settings.

4. Subscriptions & Payment Processing

Optional upgrades to TrustCheck Pro ($3.99/month) or TrustCheck Family Shield ($24.99/year) are processed directly by Google Play Billing. Barlow on Trust never receives or stores your credit card number or billing details.

5. Children's Privacy

TrustCheck does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children under the age of 13.
